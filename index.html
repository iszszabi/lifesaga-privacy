LifeSaga – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app LifeSaga (the "App"), developed by Szabi (the "Developer", "we"), handles information. We keep things simple: the App has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves. The App does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location or any other personal details, and it does not let you create an account.

2. Game data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example your current life, unlocked themes and Hall of Fame entries) are saved only on your own device. This data never leaves your device through us. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this information is deleted.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner ads, interstitial ads between lives, and optional rewarded video ads). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • your IP address (which gives an approximate location),
  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID),
  • how you interact with ads and the App,
  • diagnostic and performance information (such as crash logs).

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the Developer. You can read how Google handles it here: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices

  • Consent: Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App shows a consent form when you first open it, and you can choose how ads may use your data.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, the exact path can vary by device).
  • Remove ads: The App offers an in-game option to remove ads using in-game gems.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and contains mature themes such as references to alcohol, tobacco, crime and death. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, we do not hold any user data on our own servers. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted over encrypted connections.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, and the date above will show when it was last changed.
